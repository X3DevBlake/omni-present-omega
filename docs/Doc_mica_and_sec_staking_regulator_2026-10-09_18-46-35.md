## 1. Regulatory Executive Summary

The OmniDAO sovereign staking mechanism represents a decentralized protocol incentive rather than an investment contract under SEC v. W.J. Howey Co. Stakers deposit $OMNI tokens into non-custodial smart contracts to perform validator telemetry attestation and join-semilattice verification. Rewards represent network emission subsidies bounded by algorithmic inflation caps (max 24.8% APY).
